## idea

Файл: `assets/process-idea-pencil.webp`.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: individual illustration for a four-step website process. Style/medium: authentic hand-drawn graphite pencil on transparent background, confident slightly irregular charcoal-gray contours, visible pencil grain and variations in pressure, a few fine construction strokes and restrained diagonal hatching. Strong clean silhouette readable when reduced to about 160 px high. NOT smooth vector strokes, NOT ink, NOT a photo, no blur or messy scribbles. Drawn by the same designer with a soft 2B pencil. Only very restrained green colored-pencil accents where specified. Background must be actual transparency outside the object, with no opaque paper rectangle and no checkerboard pixels. Keep the entire object inside the canvas with a small 4% safety margin; no drop shadows, hands, captions, surrounding arrows, labels, logos or watermarks. Subject: a tall spiral-bound notebook seen almost flat from the front, leaning about 5 degrees counterclockwise. Small rings along the left binding. On its single visible page, a large simple light bulb idea sketch in the upper third, and a few rough web-wireframe rectangles, a crossed image placeholder and short horizontal pencil lines below. A page with an idea sketched on it, not a realistic product photograph. The page interior is nearly empty/translucent off-white with minimal shading around its edges, so it blends into the website's off-white paper. Graphite only. Portrait canvas, about 2:3.
